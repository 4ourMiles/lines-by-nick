## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored for LBN. The 1px-stepped navy extrusion is literally the mark's construction, the layered mark gives real depth on hover, the "Lines of ___" spine runs h1 → four h3s → footer, and the brand slab shows the studio's own identity as proof. Category-interchangeable pieces: the marquee ticker, the four numbered process cards, the browser+phone and kanban mocks, floating stack-name stickers around a hero object, a red CTA block. Missed character: "Lines by Nick" has no Nick and no drawn line beyond one scribble, although custom graphic art is a sold service.

## Overall Impression
A real brand, not a template: the first desktop viewport earns an "oh, this is real". The page peaks mid-way at the navy brand slab and dips at the end, where a red slab erases the red third of the logo. Biggest opportunity: make the phone first screen and the route selector pay off as hard as the desktop hero does.

## What's Working
- Extrusion vocabulary applied down to demo atoms, so the page is the mark at page scale, and the layered mark parallax is craft a founder can feel.
- "Lines of ___" is a structural idea that replaces icons and eyebrows and gives the four services one voice.
- Honest proof and SMB-literate copy: samples labelled, the real identity used as the brand sample, "price and timeline in writing" answers the buyer's actual fear.

## Priority Issues
- **[P1] Phone first screen has no mark.** On 390px the hero is 1,150px tall and the stage starts at y=699: a text-only cyan screen; the binding brand object is a full scroll away. Fix: below 900px render a compact stage between h1 and lede, cut hero padding to 112/64, trim the lede to one sentence, full-width CTAs. Command: /impeccable adapt
- **[P1] Route selector payoff is weak and off-screen.** Lit = 4px lift + tag; unlit slabs do not change; UX lights on every route so the answer is the same four times. Fix: unlit slabs press flat and dim, scroll the first lit slab into view or add "2 of 4" to the hint, differentiate the routes. Command: /impeccable delight
- **[P1] Sticky stack artifacts.** Two "On your route" tags pile at the same coordinate under the nav; unequal slab heights let the previous colour band show beneath the next. Fix: equal min-height for slabs, index-offset the lit tag, or drop stacking and keep the lift. Command: /impeccable polish
- **[P2] Close mark is red on red**, so the page's last image is a half-erased logo. Fix: white stage behind the mark, or a navy close slab with a red primary button. Command: /impeccable colorize
- **[P2] Ticker: category default, no pause control (WCAG 2.2.2), third repeat of the stack list.** Fix: cut it, or keep it as the only stack list and pause on hover/focus. Command: /impeccable distill
- **[P2] Contrast:** white on #ff0303 at 4.0:1 on the red sticker and swatch labels; navy-soft on cyan was fixed earlier but "Graphic design" cyan on navy reads as neon. Command: /impeccable polish
- **[P3]** Toggles 40px tall (under 44 touch target); "Why LBN" vs section headline; "Start a project" 4x; footer border invisible; `.hero__and` dead CSS; `.service` declared twice; bounce easing somewhere in the cascade; no skip link; mobile menu does not move focus.

## Persona Red Flags
- **First-time SMB owner on a phone:** text-only first screen; decoding "Lines of business / sight"; jargon chips; 9,000px page; mailto does nothing without a mail app and offers no next step; 40px toggles.
- **Skeptical founder with three tabs open:** zero real evidence beyond the studio's own logo; sample labels confirm the absence; "price in writing" with no range. Risk verdict: "the site is a mock of itself."
- **Screen reader / keyboard:** solid base (aria-pressed, live hint, hidden duplicates, focus rings), but no skip link and 25 tab stops before content, menu does not manage focus, marquee cannot be stopped, lit state is visual-first.

## Minor Observations
`section#top` measures at -14px from the nav's negative margin (harmless); close lede on red passes only because it is 20px bold; "Four kinds of line." strains next to "Lines of sight"; the mark's tints never appear on the page.
